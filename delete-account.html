Account deletion (English summary)

You can delete your Baro or Baro Pro account and personal data in two ways: (1) in the app, under Profile → Delete account (update the app if the menu is not shown yet), or (2) by emailing akswls641@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with. Use the subject "[Account deletion] Baro" or "[Account deletion] Baro Pro" and include your sign-up email (and salon name for Baro Pro). Requests are completed within 7 days and confirmed by reply.

Deleted immediately: login credentials, profile (name, email, phone number, date of birth, gender, photo), salon information, portfolio, uploaded documents, payout account and pending membership requests (Baro Pro), favorites and block lists, service preferences, notifications, push tokens, the registered payment method (also revoked at the payment provider), all unpaid service requests, and the notes and location attached to paid requests. Reviews are anonymized (author and text removed; the star rating is kept as an anonymous statistic) and any reward points are forfeited. If a salon owner account is deleted, the salon record is deleted and its staff designers are released and notified. Deletion is permanent; if a match, an additional payment or a payout is still in progress, the app shows the reason and deletion is postponed until it is completed. Accounts under an active restriction may be barred from re-registering for one year.

Retained for the period required by Korean law, separately from other data: contract and cancellation records (5 years), payment and service records limited to the name, service and amount (5 years), consumer dispute records (3 years), advertising records (6 months), tax invoices (5 years), major electronic financial transaction records (5 years; small transactions under KRW 10,000 and authorization records 1 year), access logs (3 months), location-service usage logs (6 months), booking-related chat logs shown to the other party as "deleted user" (3 years after the transaction), and the identity-verification hash with any account-restriction record (1 year after deletion, to prevent abusive re-registration).
